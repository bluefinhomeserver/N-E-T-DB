## Description

---

Loot, shoot, cast and drive in this chaotic PvP Roguelite. Level up fast, draft wild skills, and create crazy combos on the fly. Explore a GTA-style battle royale with no queues or downtime, and prepare to win [[THE FINALS|the final]] fight!

Play in a turbo-fast battle royale with infinite revives, where killing a survivor gets you back in the game. You can play BR solos, 3-man squads, or try to survive vs waves of bots in the singleplayer / coop survival mode.

DRAFT GODLIKE SKILL SYNERGIES   Fight for skill points to level up fast. Each level up gives you a choice of 2 randomly generated skills. Do you take Invisibility or Bouncing Bullets? Teleportation or Regeneration?

Like a roguelite this makes every match play different. You need to think on your feet to combo skills together and create the most broken build possible before the final showdown!
UNLOCK SKILLS & NEW CLASSES   Unlock new abilities between matches. There are over 100 unique skills, classes, and round modifers to unlock. Once unlocked you can equip skills to your build to make them appear more often in-game. Join the community to theorycraft thousands of possible skill combos!

FARM COPS, GANGS, MISSIONS & MORE   Inspired by MOBAs, the faster you farm the stronger you become. You can do missions, win supply drops, fight gangs, explore for rare loot, and if you're feeling risky - aggro the cops.
But remember, the circle is shrinking and death will set you back a lot. You have to balance risk with reward to level up as much as possible while still surviving to the final showdown.

3 ULTRA FAST ROUNDS   While other BRs have one long round, Skillshot City has 3 ultra-fast rounds - with a high-stakes showdown every 5 minutes. The winners of rounds 1 and 2 get to choose unique modifiers that affect the rest of the match, and the winner of round 3 wins the game!\
ZOMBIE REVIVALS   Your skill build progresses from round to round, and if you die you can keep playing too. You'll play as a zombie and can loot for the next round - or hunt down a survivor to revive. No queueing time, no getting booted to the main menu, just pure refined non-stop action.

OTHER COOL FEATURES     A singleplayer / coop survival mode with leaderboards and custom difficulty modifiers
Choose from 8 classes, each with a unique playstyle and strategy
Design skill builds that adjust the pool of skills you'll choose from in-game
Crazy challenges, groovy cosmetics, and stats tracking to show off your favorite skills
Small download (under 1GB) and instant join times mean you can start playing in minutes!
