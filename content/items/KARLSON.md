## Description

---

What do you get if you combine an FPS, Parkour, Slowmo & ... Milk? KARLSON. The milk-infused man will stop at nothing to locate and drink his favorite beverage.

KARLSON brings unique and skill based movement to the table, highly inspired by classic FPS titles, set in a mysterious, futuristic setting. Fight your way out of the facility, and discover the truth behind your past. And the milk.
