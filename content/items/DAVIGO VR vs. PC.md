## Description

---

VR and PC players clash in epic cross-reality combat!

Become the GIANT or a small KNIGHT and battle your friends locally or online in a variety of maps and game modes.

DAVIGO features asymmetrical multiplayer and must be played with one GIANT in VR and at least one KNIGHT on PC. KNIGHTS play for free with DAVIGO KNIGHT'S PASS.  VR / GIANT You are the boss. Unleash devastating power on the puny KNIGHTS with physics-driven, full-body gameplay. Pound the ground, rip trees and stones from the earth, and throw everything you can to leave no KNIGHT standing. PC / KNIGHT Use your arsenal to bombard the GIANT and prove that size doesn't matter. Maneuver quickly with the Grapple Beam, return incoming attacks with the Hammer, and fire Rockets to shatter the GIANT's head into pieces. Maps Battle in 7 official maps that will take you from the hellish sacrificial pit of ALTAR to the desolate, snowy peak of SUMMIT. Download custom maps created by other players to continue the fight! Game modes Face off in one of four game modes. BRAWL is DAVIGO's flagship fight to the death, while TOWER and CAPTURE spice up the battle with secondary objectives. CLASSIC recreates the DAVIGO Alpha experience and will challenge you with a limited arsenal. Modding Create custom maps in the Unity game engine with the DAVIGO Map Editor, a free Unity package that integrates with mod.io to let other players browse, download and rate your creations. Build a map of your imagination and add it to the DAVIGO universe! Features   Cross-reality combat with 1 VR player and 1-4 PC players
Local and online multiplayer
Physics-driven full-body gameplay in VR
7 maps and 4 game modes to compete in
mod.io support to download custom maps created with the DAVIGO Map Editor
Free KNIGHT'S PASS for PC players to battle VR players online
